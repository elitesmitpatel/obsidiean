# First Principles Thinking

> First principles thinking means breaking a problem down to its most basic, indisputable truths and building a new solution from scratch.

## Summary
* **Definition:** Stripping away assumptions and "how it's always been done" to look only at raw facts.
* **The Process:**
  1. Identify current assumptions.
  2. Break down the problem to its fundamental truths.
  3. Create a new solution from the ground up.

---

## Examples

### 1. SpaceX Rockets
* **Assumption:** Buying a finished rocket is too expensive ($65M+).
* **First Principle:** A rocket is just aluminum, titanium, copper, and carbon fiber. Raw materials cost only 2% of a finished rocket.
* **New Solution:** Buy raw materials and build from scratch to cut costs by 10x.

### 2. Battery Packs
* **Assumption:** Battery packs cost too much ($600/kWh) and will never be cheap.
* **First Principle:** Batteries consist of cobalt, nickel, aluminum, carbon, and polymers ($80/kWh on LME).
* **New Solution:** Combine raw materials directly to manufacture cheaper battery cells.
