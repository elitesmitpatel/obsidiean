# How To Get The Truth Without Asking Questions - Chase Hughes   [Motivational Hub](https://www.youtube.com/@MotivationalHub-365)
This video features *Chase Hughes* explaining **elicitation**, a powerful psychological technique often used by intelligence agencies like the *CIA* to gather information without raising the subject's defenses by asking direct questions (0:00-0:37).

**Core Principles of Elicitation:**
* **Avoiding Questions:** Because questions can trigger mental "security alarms," elicitation relies on making **simple statements** that encourage the other person to volunteer information by correcting you (0:00-0:06, 0:38-1:06).
* **Correcting the Record:** By stating something factually incorrect or specific, you trigger a natural human urge to provide the accurate information (1:06-1:59).
* **Disbelief:** Expressing skepticism or surprise about a detail (e.g., "There's no way that was easy") prompts the person to provide further evidence or elaborate on their experiences (2:19-3:06).
* **Bracketing:** Providing a range (e.g., "between March and May") to nudge the person toward confirming specific dates or data points (4:54-5:04).

**Strategic Phrases:**
* Using **"I bet"** (e.g., "I bet you overcame a lot to get here") or **recapping** the conversation demonstrates active listening while subtly pulling for more detail without the pressure of an inquiry (5:09-5:33).

**Key Takeaway:** The more sensitive the information you need, the fewer direct questions you should ask. By keeping the conversation feeling casual and voluntary, you lower the barrier for others to share (6:24-6:38).
