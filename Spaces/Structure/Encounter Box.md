# The Encounter Box 📦

This is my Encounter box 📦 . It shows the most recent 20 created notes in my encounter folder.

It's one of the many ways I can go through a [[Notemaking]] session.

Once I have fleshed and linked out a note to my satisfaction, I remove it from the **+ Encounters** folder.

If I can't find a way to flesh out one of these notes or link to something else, than I let it fade into the mist. #🚢
